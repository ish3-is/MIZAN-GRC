## Separation rules / قواعد الفصل

- A control owner may self-assess but may not independently approve that assessment.
- A risk owner may propose acceptance but may not approve their own acceptance.
- A remediation owner may submit completion evidence but may not independently verify closure.
- Internal Audit reviews source records and evidence without editing the original owner conclusion.
- Platform legal-review flags do not authorize a GRC user to issue legal advice.
