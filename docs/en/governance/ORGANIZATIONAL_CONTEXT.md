# Namaa Digital Solutions — organizational context

> Fictional portfolio simulation. No real organization, customer, employee or audit is represented.

Namaa Digital Solutions is a medium-sized Saudi information-technology and digital-services company with a 150-person scenario profile. It serves fictional individual and corporate customers through a customer portal, CRM, ERP, HRMS, email, identity-management and backup services. Infrastructure is hybrid cloud and on-premises. The cybersecurity team has four roles and IT has twelve roles; the seed uses a representative employee sample rather than fabricated personal profiles.

## Interested parties and expectations

| Party | Demonstration expectation |
|---|---|
| Board and executive sponsor | Risk-informed decisions, accountable ownership and accepted residual risk |
| Customers | Reliable services and appropriate protection of fictional customer data |
| Employees | Clear policies, access appropriate to duties and usable reporting channels |
| Regulators | Evidence-based applicability and accurate, non-misleading records |
| Suppliers | Defined security, privacy, evidence and offboarding requirements |
| Internal audit | Independent access to immutable source records, evidence and decisions |

## Critical services and dependencies

Customer Portal (RTO 4 hours, RPO 1 hour), Identity Management (RTO 2 hours, RPO 15 minutes) and ERP/Payroll (RTO 8 hours, RPO 4 hours) are organizational simulation objectives, not regulatory mandates. They depend on identity, network, hosting, backup, system owners and key suppliers.

## Scope and assumptions

The scope covers all eight fictional departments, the 25 seeded information assets and the five seeded suppliers. NCA ECC is used as a selected benchmark pending formal applicability. PDPL processing and territorial applicability require documented review. CSCC is inactive unless a critical-system designation is evidenced. ISO/IEC standards are voluntary unless separately adopted and licensed.

Current maturity is Initial / Informal: ownership and policies exist unevenly, evidence coverage is incomplete, several actions are overdue and no perfect-compliance claim is made.
